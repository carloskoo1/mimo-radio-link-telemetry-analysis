## MCS interpretation

The variables `mcs_dl` and `mcs_ul` correspond to **equipment-reported MCS
codes** generated by the Cambium ePMP platform.

Values such as 104, 105 or codes above 200 must not be interpreted directly
as conventional IEEE MCS indices. Their meaning corresponds to the encoding
reported by the radio equipment and firmware used during the experimental
campaign.
